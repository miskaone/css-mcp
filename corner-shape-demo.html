Key Points

  • Requires border-radius: corner-shape only works when border-radius is non-zero
  • Follows the shape: background, border, outline, box-shadow, and overflow all follow the corner shape
  • Can animate: Smoothly animates between different superellipse values
  • Individual corners: Use corner-top-left-shape, corner-top-right-shape, etc. to target specific corners
